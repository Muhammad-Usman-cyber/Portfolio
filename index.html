01 / ABOUT

Curiosity is useful.
Evidence makes it valuable.

I turn security concepts into labs, investigations, and documented technical work.

I'm pursuing a BS in Cybersecurity at Air University and building practical experience across security operations, web application security, vulnerability research, and AI security.

My SOC work includes a Splunk Enterprise lab using Sysmon telemetry, simulated attack activity, custom detection searches, and a MITRE ATT&CK-mapped investigation dashboard. In web security, I practice in authorized environments and document observed behavior, impact, and remediation rather than treating every scanner alert as a confirmed exploit.

I've also completed the TryHackMe AI Security pathway and the InfoSecLabs AI Security (The New Frontier) learning path. I build small interactive projects to turn concepts such as prompt injection and RAG security into practical learning experiences.
